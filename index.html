❓ Farmer's Field Manual

🌾 The Farming Cycle

1. Till Soil: Select the Hoe [1] and press [E] or click green grass to till fertile dirt.

2. Water Soil: Select the Watering Can [2] and water tilled dirt. Crops only grow when watered! Refill your can at the upper-right pond or well when empty.

3. Plant Seeds: Select seed packets [5] or [6] and plant them in tilled soil.

4. Harvest Crops: When crops mature and sparkle, select the Scythe [3] to harvest them into your bag!

5. Sell & Profit: Open the Market [M] or visit the wooden Shipping Bin near your cabin to sell produce for Gold.

⚠️ Hazards & Defense Tactics

🦅 Hungry Crows: Crows swoop down to eat unprotected crops! Run up and press [Space] to shoo them away. Buy a Scarecrow to protect a 5x5 area automatically.

🌿 Invasive Weeds: Wild weeds sap soil nutrients. Cut them with the Hoe or Scythe for compost!

⚡ Thunderstorms & Lightning: Lightning strikes can spark small fires! Rush with your Watering Can to extinguish the flame before crops burn.

🐾 Mischievous Moles: Bonk surfacing moles with your Hoe to chase them away and earn bonus gold!

🔥 Heatwaves: Extreme sun dries soil quickly. Keep your plots watered to prevent crops from wilting!
